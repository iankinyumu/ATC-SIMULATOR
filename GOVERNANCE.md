# Project Governance

## Maintainer

Ian Wanjiru (`@iankinyumu`) is the initial project maintainer. The maintainer reviews contributions, protects the project's licensing and source-quality standards, and coordinates releases.

## How decisions are made

Small, reversible changes can be proposed directly in a pull request. Larger changes to simulation scope, architecture, voice services, licensing, or operational behaviour should start with an issue or discussion. Keep decisions and their reasons visible in the repository so new contributors can follow the project direction.

Operational claims should include authoritative sources where possible. When a source is missing, mark the value as an assumption or placeholder and identify what review is needed. Community review is welcome; the maintainer makes the final merge decision and records major product decisions in the specification.

## Contributions and licensing

Contributions are accepted under the repository's MIT License. There is no separate contributor license agreement at this time. External data, aviation publications, voice models, and other third-party assets retain their own licenses and terms; contributors must document those before proposing inclusion.

## Project maturity

This is an early-stage prototype. The roadmap is intended to invite collaboration, not to promise dates or imply operational certification.
