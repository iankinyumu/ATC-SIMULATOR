# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Participants should help make collaboration respectful and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

* Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
* Ask questions and explain technical or operational disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
* Give useful feedback about the work, not personal attacks about the contributor.
* Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
* Be careful when discussing real-world aviation operations; distinguish verified sources from assumptions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, insults, doxxing, and disruptive or bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for conduct they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report a concern privately to the project maintainer through [@iankinyumu on GitHub](https://github.com/iankinyumu). Please include links or context where possible. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community-code pattern.
